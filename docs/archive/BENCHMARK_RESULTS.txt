## Comparison vs Governor (comparison)

### Architecture Differences
- **tokio-rate-limit**: Per-key rate limiting with DashMap lookup (real-world use case)
- **governor (direct)**: Single global rate limiter, no key lookup (simpler use case)

### Single-Threaded
- tokio-rate-limit: 70.2ns
- governor: 2.8ns (25x faster for global limiting)

### Multi-Threaded Performance
| Threads | tokio-rate-limit | governor | Ratio |
|---------|------------------|----------|-------|
| 2       | 146.1ns          | 1.9ns    | 76x   |
| 4       | 162.8ns          | 2.7ns    | 60x   |
| 8       | 352.7ns          | 20.7ns   | 17x   |
| 16      | 605.1ns          | 505.9ns  | 1.2x  |

### Key Insights
1. Governor is faster for **global rate limiting** (single limiter for all requests)
2. tokio-rate-limit excels at **per-key rate limiting** (separate limits per client/IP/user)
3. At high concurrency (16+ threads), architectures converge in performance
4. Our library provides essential multi-tenant/per-key functionality out of the box
